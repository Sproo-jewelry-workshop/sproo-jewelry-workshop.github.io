Wax Carving

Carve your own ring by hand from jeweller’s wax, and we cast it in 925 sterling silver.

€80Book for 08/12

What to expect

How the workshop goes

Wax carving is one of the oldest ways to make a ring: the shape is first made in jeweller’s wax and then turned into metal. You start with a piece of hard wax in your ring size. Anastasia shows you how to saw, file and carve it, and how to melt and drip wax with a heated tool to build up soft, organic shapes.

You can keep your ring smooth and minimal or give it a melted, textured surface, like the rings in the photos. After the workshop your ring is sent to casting in 925 sterling silver and is ready about a week later.

Step by step

Four steps

1

Sketch

Decide on the shape and texture of your ring.

2

Carve & melt

Cut and file the wax, or melt it to add texture.

3

Cast

We cast your wax model in 925 sterling silver.

4

Collect

Your silver ring is ready about a week later.

What you make

Your own silver ring, made from a wax model you carve by hand.

Silver Clay

Silver clay is a soft paste, like plasticine, that turns into real silver once fired. This time, a Christmas edition with stars and trees.

€80Book for 15/12

What to expect

How the workshop goes

Silver clay is made of tiny particles of pure silver mixed with an organic binder and water. It feels like soft plasticine, so you can roll, press, cut and add texture with your fingers and simple tools. For this Christmas edition you can shape a star, a little tree or your own design, as a pendant or a ring.

When your piece is dry, it is fired: the binder burns away and only real silver remains. Then you polish it until it shines. After about 2.5 hours you take home a finished silver piece, a perfect Christmas gift for yourself or someone you love.

Step by step

Four steps

1

Shape

Model your ring or pendant from soft silver clay.

2

Dry

Let it dry and refine the details.

3

Fire

Firing burns away the binder and leaves pure silver.

4

Polish

Polish it and take it home the same day.

What you make

A silver ring or pendant, like a star or a little tree for Christmas.
